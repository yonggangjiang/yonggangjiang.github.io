D1 Software Admin Guide

Step-by-step guidance for D1 software administrators.

Create Accounts for Newcomers

Our secretary usually sends account requests to both the administrator and the newcomer. Check the newcomer’s name and current email address.

Step 1: Request an account

Go to Domino and click “Request a new account”. Select the correct person and click “Proceed” to open the form.

Under “Email (Alias) Group(s)”, select the group that matches the newcomer’s role: d1-phd, d1-postdocs, d1-students, or d1-guest. The newcomer’s role is listed at the top of the form. Interns belong to d1-students. If the newcomer has a preferred username, you can also change “Unix login”.

Click “Request a new account”, then “Complete the request”.

Step 2: Send the account details

Wait for the account-creation email, which may take several working hours.

Download send_email.py. Fill in the three blank variables at the top of your local copy:

  • ID: your MPI email login username, without @mpi-inf.mpg.de.
  • PASSWORD: your password for IMAP/SMTP access.
  • NAME: your first name, used in the email signature.

The script searches your inbox for a subject containing both “Account Created for” and the newcomer’s name, such as [Account: Request Finished] Account Created for "Last, First".

With Python 3 installed, open a terminal in the folder containing the script and install its dependency:

python3 -m pip install imapclient

Then run the script, replacing the example name and address with the newcomer’s first name and current email address from the secretary’s request:

python3 send_email.py "First name" "newcomer@example.com"

The script uses the latest matching email to extract the account credentials, sends them to the supplied address, and attempts to save a copy in “Sent”. Check the name and recipient carefully before running it: the email is sent immediately, without a confirmation prompt.

Account Expiration

You will regularly receive emails titled “MPI-INF account expiration warning…”. If someone replies to request an extension, go to our intranet, click “IDM group manager” → “Expiry Overview”, and find the person’s account. You can then extend its expiry date. Ask the person how long an extension they need. There is no fixed approval criterion; requests are usually approved. If you notice any suspicious behavior, ask our director for advice.

Change Email Groups

Use “IDM group manager” to manage email groups such as d1-noon and d1-conspiracy. Common requests include adding someone to the noon-seminar group or creating a private group to coordinate making a graduation hat.

You may also be asked to add or remove group members. For example, the PhD hat team may ask you to remove a particular person from d1-conspiracy.

To make changes, open “IDM group manager” on our intranet, click “manage group”, and select the relevant group to edit its membership.

Attend Meetings with IST

IST regularly sends invitations to admin meetings. Coordinate with the group’s hardware administrator so that one of you attends each meeting.

Other Responsibilities

The previous administrator (Yonggang) also handled the tasks below. I suggest taking them on to ensure they remain covered:

  • D1 algorithm office hours: see our website for details. As the coordinator, you can update the website and handle requests, which are rare. You can update the website through typo3. Ask our website admin for permissions if you don’t have one. It’s always good to have editing permission to our group website - who knows, you may want to put something.

  • Microwave: regularly check that it is clean. If it is dirty, remind people to clean it after use. You can also wipe it down yourself.

  • Milk: free milk is available for our use. When the fridge runs out, ask the doorman for more.
