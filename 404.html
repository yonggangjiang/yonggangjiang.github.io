About this photo

This image comes from the science-fiction game Honkai: Star Rail. The enormous light is a human-built computing system that has undergone an intelligence explosion through recursive self-improvement (RSI). It knows everything about its world, yet remains beyond human understanding. The scientist standing before it is one of the greatest minds, and her main research challenge is now to find a way to access the machine and ask it questions. This scene captures one of her attempts, which ultimately fails.

I’ll keep this as my avatar for reasons you can probably guess, until I find a better photo of myself to replace it. Hopefully soon :)
